About StaTech

StaTech is an educational platform created to help young people develop computer science and technology skills.

Our main focus is empowering girls, especially those who may not have easy access to technology education and learning opportunities.

StaTech provides structured learning resources, lessons, quizzes, and activities that help students build their knowledge and confidence in technology.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make computer science education more accessible and to encourage young learners to see themselves as future programmers, developers, engineers, and technology leaders.

Try a local project

Explore a question from everyday life. These activities need no internet and can be done with simple materials.

Water Map water use

Gather: paper and a pencil.

  1. With permission, notice different ways water is used at home or school.
  2. Make a tally chart without writing anyone's name or private routine.
  3. Draw a bar chart and suggest one way to avoid wasting water.

Do not drink or handle water you are unsure is safe.

Farming Compare seed growth

Gather: two reused cups, soil, a few familiar seeds, and water.

  1. Ask an adult before collecting soil or seeds.
  2. Plant seeds in both cups; place one in sunlight and one in shade.
  3. Give them equal water and record changes each day for a week.

Wash your hands after handling soil. Do not eat the seeds or plants.

Health Make a handwashing guide

Gather: paper, pencils, and advice from a trusted local health worker or adult.

  1. Ask what clear steps help people wash their hands well.
  2. Draw one step per panel using simple words or pictures.
  3. Ask a few classmates whether the guide is easy to understand, without collecting names.

Share general guidance only; do not diagnose illness or collect private health details.

Weather Keep a weather diary

Gather: paper and a pencil; a thermometer is optional.

  1. For seven days, observe clouds, rain, or temperature at the same time each day.
  2. Record what you notice in a simple table.
  3. Look for patterns and draw a chart to share with your class.

Observe from a safe place. Go indoors during storms and never look directly at the sun.

Energy Find ways to save energy

Gather: paper and a pencil.

  1. With permission, notice when a room uses daylight and when it uses electric light.
  2. Make a tally over a few days without touching switches or electrical equipment.
  3. Suggest one safe way your household or school could save energy.

Ask an adult before changing anything that uses electricity.

How StaTech Works

StaTech provides a structured learning experience that allows students to learn, practise, test their knowledge, and track their progress.

1. Register

Students create a StaTech account and provide the information needed to access the learning platform.

2. Choose Your Learning

Students can explore courses and learning materials based on their grade and available subjects.

3. Learn Through Lessons

Students work through lessons and learning resources designed to make computer science and technology easier to understand.

4. Test Your Knowledge

Students complete quizzes and activities to test what they have learned.

5. Track Your Progress

The student dashboard keeps track of completed lessons, quizzes, results, and overall learning progress.

6. Earn Achievements

Students can work towards achievements and learning milestones as they progress through StaTech.
